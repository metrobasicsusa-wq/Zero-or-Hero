# Independent option-feasibility protocol notes

Offline protocol review only. No network request, quote acquisition, order, simulation or old-file change was performed by this review. The prior proposal, historical-access logs and cached exchange/OCC evidence were inspected. Existing 401/404 observations concern particular requests; they do not establish the cause of failure or the absence of all historical option data.

1. **Freeze the capability sample and its denominator.** Fix the 2026 start, last completed product-specific session, time zone, sample dates, underlying/product strata, call/put and expiry strata, observation times, pagination/retry caps and total request budget before reading the new observations. Include older expired contracts across the YTD months and a recent control; do not replace failed cells with successful dates/contracts. A bounded sample spanning months is not exhaustive YTD coverage. Report each requested cell, including unsupported products, unknown listing coverage, entitlement failure, empty response, pagination truncation and malformed data. A selected set of ETFs or stocks is a declared capability sample, not a broad strategy universe.

2. **Separate historical existence, discoverability and current metadata.** Enumerating today's active contracts, querying today's expired/inactive records, and retrieving one guessed OCC symbol answer different questions. Verify default expiry/status filters, expired-contract access, complete pagination and whether listing/start dates or an actual historical as-of contract master are supplied. An archive lookup can prove a returned contract existed; it does not prove the historical chain was complete or discoverable then. Preserve unresolved historical membership and delisted/renamed/adjusted roots. Do not generate historical strikes from today's chain or current underlying price and call them a complete historical universe.

3. **Keep any sampling strike/expiry choice causal.** If the quote sample chooses ATM or neighboring strikes, freeze tie-breaks and select against a timestamped underlying observation available at the declared decision time. If historical discovery cannot be certified, label the resulting quotes as conditional on the supplied contract identifiers. Do not use daily final close/volume, a later option premium, a later Greek, a future best spread, or surviving current contracts to select a supposedly historical opportunity. Feasibility findings should not select a return strategy or filter the retained prior equity candidates.

4. **A returned quote is an observation, not a fill.** Preserve OPRA versus indicative/delayed feed identity, event versus reception time, exact timestamp precision, corrections and quote conditions. Freeze maximum age, quote-side eligibility, zero bid, locked/crossed quotes, exchange and size units before a pricing study. Entry uses the relevant ask and exit the relevant bid as conditional reference assumptions; displayed size is not guaranteed capacity. A last quote before a target time needs an age limit; a first quote after the time is not contemporaneously known. Verify all raw pages, bounds and tokens. Minute OHLC, latest snapshots, theoretical prices and isolated successful requests cannot substitute for missing historical bid/ask/size or certify continuous exit coverage.

5. **Whole-contract affordability includes the contract specification and fees.** For a verified standard contract, require integer quantity q and q × multiplier × entry ask + entry fees + the frozen cash reserve ≤ settled cash. Select an intended quantity before execution and retain any later affordability failure without resizing or switching contracts unless that policy is separately registered. Do not assume every deliverable is 100 ordinary shares: adjusted contracts can include different shares or cash; unresolved deliverables are unsupported. Freeze per-contract, exchange, regulatory, exercise/assignment and closing costs with the relevant effective-date scope. Stock basis-point costs and fractional contracts are unsuitable substitutes. A displayed $4.99 option can exceed a $500 all-in budget.

6. **Expiration is a separate funded-account dependency.** Record exact last-trading time, expiration and settlement calendar, multiplier/deliverable, American versus European exercise, cash versus physical settlement, and AM versus PM settlement for the actual series. Standard SPX, SPXW, XSP and SPY are not interchangeable. Physical long calls can require strike × deliverable cash; long puts can require shares or create a short position. Early assignment, exercise-by-exception, contrary instructions/DNE deadlines and broker risk liquidation need verified rules. A planned pre-expiry exit needs valid exit evidence; a missing bid does not justify an invented sale, intrinsic-value cash settlement, or automatic zeroing that removes possible share exposure.

7. **Debit spreads need both legs and account handling.** Freeze complete contract identities, same expiry/deliverable assumptions, synchronized executable sides or a supported complex-order reference, leg counts and all-leg fees. Different-time midpoints do not demonstrate an executable net debit. A theoretical bounded expiration payoff does not settle asymmetric exercise, early assignment, pin risk, residual stock, broker permissions or margin. Keep entry debit, unsettled proceeds, exercise liabilities and any residual assets in a continuous ledger; do not treat credit from an unfilled short leg as cash financing the long leg.

8. **Keep conclusions at the demonstrated level.** Separate endpoint/schema correctness, authentication/entitlement, historical contract discovery, quote archive availability and account-execution feasibility. The current stage should produce a capability matrix and explicit unknowns, not a $500-to-$10,000 probability, a leverage-transformed equity return or an implied restart policy. All inspected 2026 history remains exploratory; a future strategy requires its own small frozen family and unseen validation.
