# Retrospective event anatomy, not a new trading rule

The parent study's 11 dates with complete SPY-down primary outcomes were already known. Coverage reconstruction found a twelfth date, June 18, with three SPY-down selected stocks but no complete primary target outcome. This stage retains all original selected cases on all twelve dates, with `date_role` distinguishing outcome-support from no-complete-outcome diagnostic. The initial eleven-date scope files remain archived with hashes. Annual coverage still has all 191 dates: eleven with complete outcomes, one with selected SPY-down cases but no complete outcomes, and 179 without selected SPY-down cases. No date is silently scored as zero profit.

All 1,227 cases and 44,172 original parameter rows are retained. Parent signals, entry/exit prices, net returns, matched peers, costs and missingness are immutable. Selected benchmarks remain separate from stock statistics. The primary concentration calculation uses **only** the fixed 2%/REBOUND/60-minute/10-bp-per-side SPY-down signals, including missing outcomes; complete denominators must reconcile to the prior 153 target returns and 134 matched-excess observations. This does not produce a portfolio or permit simultaneous use of the same $500 for every signal.

First-30-minute features use only unique valid bars 0–29 and become complete at 10:00 New York time. The previous close is inherited from the registered prior-session metric; it is a raw historical provider value, with adjustment and revision limitations. A forced ETF has no supplied previous-close metric, so its gap remains unknown while its available intraday features are retained. No extra daily-price query or substituted prior close is used. First-30 volume is a raw sum, not same-time relative volume. Entry/decision/exit times are stored separately as later strategy behavior, never represented as information available at 10:00.

The two return-feature bins are fixed at ≤−2%, between −2% and zero, and ≥zero, with unknown separate. Price cross-products/exact rational ratios preserve boundary equality rather than using rounded display returns. Trough-minute bins are 0–9, 10–19 and 20–29. Bin denominators include all primary SPY-down selected cases, including no-flush/no-rebound/unknown. Feature summaries grouped by the eventual outcome sign are explicitly conditioned on known outcomes; they do not show predictive ability. Thresholds are not optimized or used to create new entries.

Concentration separates target net profit from relative matched excess. Each has its own observed event count N and nonempty-date count D. Pooled entity contribution is its sum divided by original N. Date-equal contribution sums each event divided by its original date count and then original D. Contributions across dates, symbols and date-symbol cells must exactly reconstruct the respective overall mean before decimal display rounding.

Leave-one-date/symbol-out recomputes the remaining available event and date denominators, lists dates that lose all observations, and never rematches peers or changes another event's return. Both favorable and unfavorable entities are checked. This is a retrospective sensitivity calculation, not validation, an exclusion recommendation, or a reason to delete large winners from the main sample. The user accepts low-win-rate asymmetric payoff research; there is no 40% win-rate requirement. Tail dependence alone neither proves nor disproves positive expectancy.

The official macro inventory covers BLS CPI/PPI/Employment Situation, BEA GDP vintages/Personal Income and Outlays, and FOMC meetings/statements. FOMC minutes were additionally checked as a clearly labeled background category; they are not a preregistered return split or an added trading feature. Current official schedules and page embargo labels cannot establish historical first delivery or strategy receipt. Releases at 10:00 are not automatically available before a 10:00 decision. “Not listed in the current official inventory” does not mean no news. Event existence is not attribution for price movements. Full official pages remain private; public evidence includes URLs, source hashes, capture times and short quotations.

This is an outcome-informed historical diagnostic. Current-directory survivorship, historical revisions, missing-minute observability, selected dates and stock-specific trough timing remain limitations. No new confidence interval, fitted prediction, executable option return, ruin probability, restart path or $10,000 success probability is calculated. The $500 PAPER-round goal and the requirement to count all failures, residual funds and future reinjections remain unchanged.
