## Minimum specification to freeze

1. Retain prior-close >= $2 and prior-20-session median dollar volume >= $10 million, using previously adjusted references without adjusting splits twice. Recheck the actual regular-session opening gap >= 10% from the exact opening bar. Later current-session high, close or full volume cannot determine an early signal.
2. For N in {5,15,30}, the range is offsets 0 through N−1, complete at 09:30 + N. Freeze the volume gate before inspecting outcomes. Keeping cumulative V_N / prior20 mean full-session volume >= 1.0 for all arms is the smallest comparison that retains the original liquidity threshold. A gate >= N/30 is a separate linear-time research assumption; it is neither measured same-time relative volume nor an empirically verified intraday volume curve. Do not choose between these definitions after seeing their performance.
3. Freeze one common signal deadline: test completed-minute closes at offsets N through 89, strictly above the completed OR_N high. If a signal minute begins at offset m, it completes at m+1; one full minute of latency yields the exact opening reference at m+2. The earliest OR5 entry is 09:37. The last reference needed is offset 91 (11:01). The existing cache extends to offset 92.
4. A missing or invalid opening-range bar makes that arm unknown under the original strict gate model. During signal search, the first missing preceding minute prevents certification of the first breakout. A later missing minute must not retroactively remove an already confirmed signal. Do not filter down to candidates whose entire future input window happens to be complete. The holding-only provider event assumption must not silently become an opening-range or signal-volume reconstruction rule.
