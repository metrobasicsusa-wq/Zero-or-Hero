These counts include overlapping variants and are not independent market observations.

Those 12 provider-assumption retrospective portfolios all have conditional endpoint marks below $500 and null realized profit. None recorded a valid daily $1,000 milestone. All 12 provider-mode entered-and-closed independent-case mean PnLs are negative; that selected subset does not estimate every opportunity or future returns.

Before actual returns, source-conflict wiring, detail fields and uncertain candidate precedence were hardened and retested. Version-conflicted cases remain members and become data-quality unknowns, with conservative whole-window quarantine; missing future request data instead fail at time of use. The actual input set has neither issue. These are research data rules, not historical live execution events.

OR5/15/30 changes qualification and stops together with timing. All current-pool, daily-open prescreen, source-clock/version, action-mapping, OHLC execution, retrospective-ready-cohort and previously inspected-history limitations remain. Unknown paths are not financial losses or bankruptcy. No funding reset, actual trade, scheduler or separate main-account change was performed.
