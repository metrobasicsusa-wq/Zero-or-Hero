# Market-relative opening-flush exploratory study

The frozen `study-design.json` governs classification and primary inference. Every case and all 36 execution/cost variants from parent commit `634a93c65e1adec6c5908f9ad249c5b8e6224ba8` remain in scope. No new market-data query, entry, exit or peer selection is introduced. Unknown observations are retained, not imputed as zero. Classification is known only after the first 30 minutes finish at 10:00 New York time.

The two known groups mean that the selected benchmark was, or was not, down at least 0.5% at the target stock's earliest minimum-close minute in the first 30 minutes. They are not causal labels for market or company news. SPY is primary; QQQ is a separately reported sensitivity, never a substitute chosen after seeing its result. Because each stock selects its own trough minute, even same-date groups can differ in trough and entry times. Descriptive signal-minute distributions are therefore reported as a confounding diagnostic, without adjusting or reselecting cases afterward. Same-date comparisons further condition on both groups having complete original three-peer comparisons.

Profitability metrics use complete **target net returns**, including the inherited cost scenario exactly once. Positive matched excess does not necessarily mean a profitable trade. Every group reports all cases, eligible/unknown flush status, signal/no-flush/no-rebound/unknown counts, return missingness and complete-pair counts. Unknown classification is its own group. No minimum 40% win-rate screen applies.

`return_distribution.py` uses exact rational arithmetic for the observed expectation identity and returns JSON-safe decimal strings. Average winning and losing amounts are conditional on their respective signs. The empirical break-even rate L/(G+L) is conditional on nonzero outcomes and on observed magnitudes remaining fixed. It is not a forecast. Zeros are separately counted; an undefined statistic has a null value and reason. Positive and negative empirical 5th/95th quantiles and worst-tail diagnostics are descriptive risk additions, not new filters or order rules. All tails remain in the main mean.

Top 1%/5% diagnostics choose at most ceil(q*Ncomplete) positive events, ranked by observed return and stable case ID, with actual removed count recorded. Their positive-profit share divides by all positive gains, not by total net profit that may be near zero or negative. The mean after removing selected tails only measures dependence on extremes retrospectively. It is not an executable rule for identifying winners early. No date-weighted tail-removal estimate is used.

All 36 variants, both benchmarks and all three groups receive full-period, monthly and H1/H2 descriptions. SPY/QQQ benchmark targets remain in the joined ledger as not-stock targets. Daily summaries retain all 191 calendar dates for every stock group and variant. The primary variant remains 2% decline, REBOUND, 60-minute holding, 10 bp per side. Its group net means, matched-excess means, and same-date not-down-minus-down matched-excess contrast receive 2,000-date bootstrap intervals. Other variants have descriptive contrast point estimates, not additional intervals. Fewer than two complete dates yields no interval. The SHA256 seed namespace and linear percentile rule are frozen in the design.

Negative-day runs use the primary group's date-equal target net return. A nonnegative or missing calendar date breaks an observed run; missing dates are explicitly reported. Simultaneous or overlapping signals are not arbitrarily ordered into a trade losing streak. This is not $500 account survival, margin capacity or drawdown.

The parent outcome summaries were already known before this follow-up. This is exploratory historical stratification, not untouched out-of-sample validation. Current-universe survivorship, retrospective revisions, complete-minute selection, limited liquidity matching and non-independent dates remain limitations. No historical executable option bid/ask, capital ledger, new account operation or scheduler is supplied. The user's view that 0DTE win rate may be below 40% is recorded as a preference to examine asymmetric payoffs, not asserted as an empirically verified rate.
