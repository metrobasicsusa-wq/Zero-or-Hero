# Independent primary-source audit

All 29 newly positive classifications and their 31 primary source bodies passed independent SHA-256, recorded-byte-length and declared/displayed timestamp checks. Each announcement and the conservative available-by time is strictly after the prior regular close and before the candidate regular open. The four group-file hashes match the frozen 68-row aggregate; the audit changed no classifications.

Nine of the 31 sources have no declared revision time. Current source bytes and publication metadata do not establish an immutable historical version, first public availability or a trading client's receipt. These are category-and-declared-time assessments, not proof of favorable news or executable opportunity.

VELO's issuer page has a timezone-free time; the separate original PRNewswire timestamp supplies the explicit timezone and later declared revision. AMBQ's issuer and wire declarations differ by five hours; both are in the overnight window, and the later wire time controls. CAPR and IMVT show explicit EDT release timestamps in the visible body. These exceptions were inspected directly.

CRM's cached body does not match its earlier metadata hash. The observed and expected hashes are preserved in the JSON audit. It was quarantined before the classification lock, contributes no positive proof, and CRM remains unknown. The audit does not silently repair that cache.

The primary classification lock precedes the first recorded exit-simulation start. This verifies saved ordering and current hashes; it cannot establish an external immutable event log. Unknown and known outside-category stories do not establish absence of another qualifying catalyst.

Re-run the offline checks with `python audit_primary_sources.py` when the matching private source archives are available. The public JSON includes source URLs, hashes, timestamp evidence methods and caveats, not full article bodies.
